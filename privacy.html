Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Inkwell does not collect any data. Nothing you write, dictate, or do inside Inkwell is sent to us, to any analytics service, or to any advertiser. There is no account to create and no server this app talks to that we operate.

What stays on your device

Your journal entries, the app's on-device reflections on them, voice dictation, and all app settings are stored only on your device, using Apple's standard on-device storage and on-device Speech and AI frameworks. We never see this content, because it never leaves your device to reach us.

iCloud sync

If you enable iCloud sync, your entries are synced through your own personal iCloud account, using Apple's CloudKit framework. That data goes to your iCloud, under your control, subject to Apple's own privacy policy for iCloud — not to Inkwell's developer. We have no access to it.

Third parties

Inkwell contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking software of any kind.

Children's privacy

Since Inkwell collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the contact link on the support page.
