Inkwell Support

Inkwell is a private journal that lives entirely on your device — voice or typed entries, an on-device reflection after you save, and nothing sent anywhere except your own iCloud if you turn sync on.

Having a problem?

If something isn't working — a crash, a feature that doesn't behave the way you expect, dictation acting up — please describe what happened (what you tapped, what you expected, what happened instead) and, if you can, when it happened, so it's easier to trace through your device's own logs.

Email vw0226apple@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

Does Inkwell collect my data? No. Everything — your entries, dictation, and the on-device reflection — stays on your device. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Where do my entries go if I turn on iCloud sync? To your own personal iCloud account only, through Apple's CloudKit framework. We never see it.

Why does dictation need on-device Speech Recognition? So your voice is transcribed without leaving your device, consistent with everything else Inkwell does.
